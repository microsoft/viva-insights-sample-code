# Comparable process footprints

Use the core conventions for units, weighting, distinct-person privacy and
causal claims. Prefer installed package functions after verifying signatures.
The custom work here is defining scope and comparison, not replacing standard
aggregation or plotting APIs.

## Compare the same observed question

Keep full history, full historical cycles, matched current cycle-to-date,
calendar-year context and any separate PQ window explicit. A partial current
cycle must be compared with the same observed phase in earlier years.
Do not compare a full historical peak with only the opening of a current cycle.
Matched calendar dates reduce seasonal differences but do not eliminate them.

Use verified source coverage before assigning zeros. Track observed days/weeks,
missingness and eligible population alongside totals. Separately assess whether
capture, population, topic naming or classification changed between years.

## Metrics and denominators

Start with meetings, exported attendee hours and mean attendee hours per meeting.
The exact count-times-mean identity separates volume from time investment per
event. Define the allocation of any interaction in a change decomposition.
Use duration and attendance as additional context; their separate means need
not multiply to exported attendee hours.

For each available behavioural metric, record numerator, valid denominator,
units and weighting. Message counts per attendee are not fractions or rates per
minute unless explicitly divided by duration. Binary multitasking flags do not
measure attention quality or minutes distracted.

Compute standalone categories, intersections and the exact union. Reconcile
all = included + outside on the same quality-filtered envelope. Normalised
process share needs both its numerator and the all-meeting denominator.
A small percentage-point change can still be a substantial relative change.

## Organisational lens

MQ attributes identify the organiser's recorded reporting line. They do not
identify the process owner or the organisations of everyone attending. State
attribute vintage and attribution coverage before interpreting movements.

Keep raw keys for joins. Clean display labels separately. Do not merge a leader
succession, spelling variant or malformed label into an alias without evidence.
An observed-year eligibility failure is unavailable, not a zero or -100% change.

Keep reportable, below-threshold and unattributed totals reconciled privately.
Apply distinct-person privacy, complementary protection and event stability as
separate gates. Many meetings from one organiser do not establish a safe group.
If the export cannot establish the relevant person base, withhold or aggregate.

Use a common category set and stable colours for comparison charts. A treemap
conditions on its stated denominator within each year. Show missing attribution
or state its exclusion, and label any pooled categories.

## Optional person-side evidence

Confirm a custom PQ process metric's definition and source vintage. It need not
match an MQ subject classifier. Validate its unit, grain, parent-metric bounds,
missingness and measurement changes before interpretation.

Keep summed hours separate from equal-person means per observed/active week.
State eligibility and any latest-label assignment across the whole window.
Observed active zero weeks belong in the agreed denominator; unresolved coverage
does not. A changed organisational ranking can reflect reassignment or changing
membership. Do not label it behavioural growth without a compatible comparison.

Keep MQ and PQ separate unless a justified join has verified keys, grain and
cardinality. Never sum their hours together or use a recent PQ population as an
unstated denominator for historical organiser-attributed MQ hours.

## Monitoring, inference and scenarios

Describe effect sizes and uncertainty without equating meeting activity with
productivity, audit quality, compliance or realised savings. Avoid person or
leader performance rankings.

For requested tests, choose a design, dependence structure and multiple-testing
family first. Temporal blocks or recurrent organiser/series clusters can matter.
Report practical thresholds as agreed reporting choices, not approved success
targets. Non-significance does not prove equivalence.

A projection from historical shapes requires comparable coverage and an explicit
scenario assumption. Historical spread is not automatically a confidence interval.
The export does not reveal future scheduled meetings. Distinguish elapsed but
unobserved time from future time. No generic projection/inference runner is
provided by this skill.
