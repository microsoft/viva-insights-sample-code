Definitions & coverage

Scope: the Person Query roster and the declared 13-week synthetic product window, 5 April to 4 July 2026. Headcount is the roster, not the sum of service user counts. Non-roster agent records are excluded from the dashboard.

Missing coverage: no confirmed unlicensed Copilot Chat activity, other AI vendors, satisfaction survey, task-completion outcomes or output-success measures are supplied. “AI users” means users observed in the supplied sources, not all AI use in the organisation.

Synthetic scenario: reproducible staggered team rollouts, changing weekly participation and demand, and a temporary dip with recovery. These changes are applied to source records, not chart lines. Access and HR attributes are unchanged. Existing licensed-activity gaps remain unresolved. The agent scenario assumes no use before its first week; committed CSVs and other demos are unchanged.

Metric definitions and denominator rules
Observed AI users
A distinct person with positive M365 Copilot actions or active days, Cowork tasks or active days, agent sessions, metered M365 sessions or credits, or GitHub activity or credits during the window. A person using multiple services is counted once. A positive observation remains positive even if another source is unresolved.
Recorded activity per person per week
Recorded actions, tasks, sessions, feature uses or credits divided by all roster person-weeks in the selected period: headcount multiplied by weeks. Non-users, unlicensed people and person-weeks with activity gaps stay in the denominator. Only received activity contributes to the numerator; rates are recorded lower bounds where coverage is incomplete, not estimates of missing usage. Group rates use that group’s full roster; agent, feature and service breakdowns use the full organisation roster, not only that category’s users. Raw totals remain in audit details.
M365 Copilot access and adoption
Access uses positive Total Copilot enabled days in the latest week, not activity. Licensed zeros remain in the denominator. The latest-week adoption rate is published only when activity is known for every licensed person and the licence and activity counts meet disclosure thresholds. Native licensed-only M365 nulls mean unlicensed under Microsoft’s documented rules, but these historical fixtures deliberately contain processing gaps in licensed activity. Those gaps remain unresolved. Their unlicensed zeros are fixture conventions, not the native-export null convention.
GitHub use and acceptance
Recorded use includes positive suggested or accepted completions, user-initiated chats, the Agent adoption indicator, or GitHub AI credits. A missing activity row is not a licence record or proof of non-use. Acceptance is total accepted completions divided by total suggested completions over the received records, not an average of person-level percentages. Zero suggestions produce no rate.
Returning agent users
The share of distinct active agent users in the latest week whose exported seven-day returning indicator is one. Repeated person-level indicators are deduplicated across agents. They are never added across weeks. This is not retention across all AI services.
Credit volume
M365 Copilot credits and GitHub AI credits retain separate units. Credits per roster person-week divide recorded credits by all roster person-weeks in the selected window, including people with no activity or no licence. Daily records are aggregated to Sunday-start weeks before joining to the roster. Feature and agent facts remain separate so they cannot multiply the person-week panel. No currency conversion, licence fees, policy-limit totals or savings are inferred.
Collaboration activity and credit levels
Emails sent, Teams chats sent, meetings attended and collaboration hours come from the Person Query. Collaboration hours include overlapping meeting and other collaboration activity only once; do not add hours to the other measures. Metric rules can vary by organisation. Credit groups use each person’s average recorded weekly credits in the selected period. People with zero recorded credits form one group; positive values are divided using organisation-wide tercile cutoffs, with ties kept together. A band can be empty. Filters retain those cutoffs and use each person’s latest group in the selected period. M365 and GitHub credits are never combined. Missing credit observations make that product’s group comparison unavailable. Missing activity remains in the denominator: “At least” marks a recorded lower bound, while all-missing values are unavailable. These are associations within the same period, not causal or predictive results.
Disclosure and interpretation
Some aggregates are withheld under a minimum group size of 10 people and complementary suppression rules. These rules do not prevent inference by comparing overlapping time windows, credit bands, teams or other linked aggregates. This synthetic example is not an approved real-export adapter or a privacy guarantee. Real use requires mapping, coverage, organisation-specific disclosure review and suitable controls. Tool activity does not establish productivity, satisfaction or causal impact.
