🔒 Responsible use & data privacy

  • Suppress small groups. Apply the stricter of your organisation's minimum group size and the selected workflow's privacy floor to every breakdown, segment and chart.
  • Never expose individuals. Do not print raw UPNs or email addresses. Hash, truncate or report only aggregates. Person Query PersonId is already de-identified, but Purview UserId is not.
  • Report group patterns. Use these outputs to understand cohorts and trends. Do not evaluate, rank or monitor named employees.
  • Keep data in approved environments. Follow your organisation's policies when choosing an agent and deciding which data it may access. Validate every agent output before sharing.
